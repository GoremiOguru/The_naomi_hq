Biography & Story

One person. One story. One perspective at a time.

Storytelling has been central to my work for more than a decade. It has shown me how people make sense of their experiences, how labels can limit what they see in themselves, and how a different perspective can open a possibility they had not considered.

The Sparkle Queen

An affectionate moniker given to Naomi by global audiences and communities for her irrepressible energy, luminous warmth, and gift for bringing clarity to complex spaces.

Evolution & Conviction

Connecting Strategy, Storytelling and Human Value

“Changing the world, one story at a time” was an early expression of that conviction. Today, it also informs my work in strategic communication, visibility, leadership and human potential.

Over the course of my career, I observed a recurring pattern across corporate boardrooms, public stages, and personal conversations: remarkable leaders and visionary organisations often struggle to articulate their most distinctive value. They get trapped behind conventional jargon or allow rigid labels to dictate their impact.

“My purpose remains consistent across every stage, boardroom, and conversation: to reveal possibilities.”

Today, I serve as the Chief Strategist of The CentreStage Company, working with institutions and leaders on positioning, communication clarity, thought leadership and reputation. I am also the creator of The Irreplaceable Advantage™—a methodology helping individuals and organisations discover, develop, and apply what is uniquely theirs in a rapidly changing world.

Professional Evolution

Framework & Innovation
