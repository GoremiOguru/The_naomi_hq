Meet Naomi

One person. One story. One perspective at a time.

Connecting strategy, storytelling and human potential.

Storytelling has been central to my work for more than a decade. It has shown me how people make sense of their experiences, how labels can limit what they see in themselves, and how a different perspective can open a possibility they had not considered.

“Changing the world, one story at a time” was an early expression of that conviction. Today, it also informs my work in strategic communication, visibility, leadership and human potential.

I am the Chief Strategist of The CentreStage Company, founder of The Women With Stories and creator of The Irreplaceable Advantage™. Across these expressions, my purpose remains consistent: to reveal possibilities.

Proprietary Framework

The Irreplaceable Advantage™

What is uniquely yours to see, say, solve and shape?

Your value includes how you interpret experience, exercise judgement, connect ideas and respond to the world around you.

I created The Irreplaceable Advantage™ to help people discover, develop and apply that distinctive combination. As technology changes how work gets done, this understanding becomes increasingly relevant to the way we communicate, lead and contribute.
