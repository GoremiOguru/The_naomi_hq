Legal & Data Handling

Privacy Policy

How Naomi Osemedua and TheNaomiHQ collect, protect, and respect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an engagement enquiry through TheNaomiHQ.com, we collect the details you voluntarily provide: your name, business or personal email address, organisation name, proposed event date, audience profile, and your project description.

2. Purpose of Processing

The information submitted is used solely to evaluate potential speaking, panel moderation, conversation hosting, event compering, strategic advisory, media appearances, or institutional partnerships. We do not sell, rent, or trade your contact information with external marketers.

3. Data Retention & Storage

Enquiry data is retained only for the duration necessary to communicate with you and coordinate upcoming engagements. Client records for contracted engagements are retained in accordance with professional accounting and corporate legal obligations through The CentreStage Company.

4. Cookies & Privacy-First Analytics

This website uses privacy-respecting analytics that do not track personal identities or transmit personal enquiry contents in analytics event payloads. You can adjust your browser settings at any time to refuse cookies.

5. Contacting the Data Controller

If you have any questions regarding how your data is handled or wish to request the deletion of your correspondence, please contact the executive team at executive@thenaomihq.com.
